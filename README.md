## About

I work on physical-layer algorithms for 5G and next-generation wireless systems, with a focus on waveform design, PAPR reduction, and machine learning for the air interface. My experience spans link-level and system-level simulation, BER/SNR analysis, and algorithm development in MATLAB and Python. I am particularly interested in power- and complexity-efficient baseband solutions for modem design.
